# 02 · Voice of the user (2025–2026)

## Pain points, ranked
1. **"Unlimited" isn't.** Only some models, web only, throttled into a slow queue; labels changed after purchase. (Trustpilot 1★ pages, Reddit Feb 2026, Forbes)
2. **Cost is invisible until it's spent; credits expire.** Cost varies by model/length/resolution; re-rolls add up; packs expire after 90 days. A reviewer burned 200+ credits before learning which models were expensive.
3. **Refunds, auto-renewal, cancellation.** 7–8 of 20 sampled 1★ reviews. Five retention offers to cancel (Taylor Lorenz, Jul 2026).
4. **Slow queues, stuck jobs.** Hours-long waits reported; a reviewer timed normal clips at 2–18 min.
5. **Upsell pop-ups and dark patterns** before the first generation.
6. **Bad results still cost full price.** Off-prompt takes are charged; every edit re-renders the whole clip. (Truly failed jobs are refunded.)
7. **Character / likeness drift** (mixed — some praise Soul ID).
8. **Too much to navigate** (weak–medium): confusing model names, disconnected sections.
9. **Trust / marketing controversies** (press).

## What users love, ranked
1. Every top model in one place.
2. Output quality, especially cinematic video.
3. **Camera control** (Cinema Studio, presets) — the clearest differentiator in reviews, more a marketing hook than a praised daily tool.
4. Character consistency, when it works.
5. Easy start (first video in ~5 min).

## Who and why
~15M users, ~300k paying (Forbes). Jobs: fast social content; ads/UGC at volume with a consistent "actor"; short films and music videos; trying new models without five subscriptions.

## Three "better than the original" ideas (from the evidence)
1. **Show cost and wait before Generate** — exact cost, what the balance still buys, ETA, auto-refund history.
2. **Cheap draft before expensive final** — low-res drafts, pick one, then render the final; live job tray; cancel while queued.
3. **A calm, intent-first studio** — camera-move gallery as the entry point; models as Fast/Best/Cheap; no upsell before the first generation.
