The prior plan's storyboard ([QA-AND-DEMO.md](recon/prior-plan/QA-AND-DEMO.md#walkthrough-storyboard--target-430)), adapted to Dailies. Record on the production URL in a fresh signed-out browser window. Speak plainly; the bracketed lines are what to show.

|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | [Camera on you, then the live link] | "This is Dailies, my rebuild of Higgsfield. It's for a solo creator who wants a cinematic camera move on a still image, without paying per try or learning models." |
