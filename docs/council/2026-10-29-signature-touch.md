# Council: the signature touch (29 Oct 2026, 02:25 UTC)

Run with the `llm-council` skill: five advisors answered independently, five reviewers ranked their anonymised answers, a chairman wrote the verdict. The owner asked for the verdict to be built as decided.

**Question.** What single touch, buildable in about 2.5 hours, would make Dailies the rebuild graders remember, and what should we not do?

| Advisor | Proposal | Reviewers' verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Executor | **Witness cam**: a second, fixed camera films the shot camera flying through the photo's relief, in a corner of the player | Strongest for 3 of 5: shows what a generated video can't (the camera), additive, same WebGL context, export provably untouched |
| First Principles | **Contact sheet**: the move picker becomes nine live loops of your own frame | Strongest for 2 of 5; but /moves already shows nine live previews, so it mostly moves an existing feature |
| Outsider | Drop or paste any image anywhere and it comes alive | Biggest blind spot: your own photo needs a 27–50 MB model first, so the moment becomes a progress bar |
| Expansionist | "Your Move": perform the camera move by dragging, share it in a URL | Rewrites the deterministic core five hours before freeze; hand-drawn paths reveal holes in a 2.5D scene |
| Contrarian | Revert the reskin, or burn a slate into the exported MP4 | Reopens a decided reskin; the slate only appears after export |

**Blind spots the review caught.** A steep side view of a displaced plane shows stretched "rubber-sheet" edges, so draw the relief as points from a raised three-quarter angle. Browsers cap live WebGL contexts at about 16, so the touch adds none. It needs a kill switch, a go/no-go time, a reduced-motion and a phone fallback, and a moment in the re-recorded video.

**Verdict: build the witness cam.** On by default in the Explore hero; a "Witness cam" toggle and the W key on the Studio preview; off on takes and never in export. Kill switch: `WITNESS_ENABLED` in `src/lib/flags.ts`, or `?witness=0`. Go/no-go 04:30 UTC: passed at 02:30 (all checks below).

**Not built:** the contact sheet, the burned-in slate, Your Move, paste-anywhere, share links, a draggable witness view, keyframes, a tenth move, more AI generation.
